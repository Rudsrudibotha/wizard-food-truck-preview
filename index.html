Something for every appetite

Breakfast to late bites, no one left out.

Breakfast

Start your day with a big breakfast burrito stuffed with crispy hashbrowns, scrambled eggs, salsa, your choice of meat and cheese, plus waffles and breakfast sandwiches.

Vegan & vegetarian

Vegetarian tacos, taco meals and taco salad bowls with vegan meat, plus a plant-based burger (just let us know ahead of your event).
